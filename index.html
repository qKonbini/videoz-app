VIDEOZ

VIDEOZ is a private video production tool used by the creator Kao (qKonbini). It edits Kao's own gameplay recordings into short vertical videos and uploads them to Kao's own YouTube channel.

VIDEOZ is not a public service. Nobody else can sign in to it or use it.

What it does with YouTube

  • Uploads videos to the owner's channel, with the privacy setting the owner chose.
  • Reads that channel's own video status and statistics, to check uploads and measure results.
